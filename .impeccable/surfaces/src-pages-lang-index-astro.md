## Scope

Whole site (home, sobre mí, proyectos, ficha de proyecto, CV, contacto, 404). Visitor mode: **Persuade** on home (a productora or comité decides to read, contact or download), **Read** on project/CV pages. Visual world pinned by the user: their own previous site https://freyaabaad.wixsite.com/portfolio, rebuilt "mismo mundo, mejor oficio". No concept roll: a user-pinned world beats the roll.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A writer's notebook in peach and cobalt, not a template. The site alternates whole bands of peach paper and drenched cobalt like chapter breaks, and every section opens with a real screenplay slugline. It refuses the dark-cinema, film-strip portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Peach #FFE3D0 ground, cobalt #0262DE drench bands, taupe #CCB6A6 panels, near-black ink. Thin geometric caps in Jost 300 with wide tracking for display, Barlow 300/400 for body, Courier Prime only for sluglines and screenplay notation. Authored motifs: a twelve-ray stroke asterisk, plus a filled scalloped badge star and a filled four-point sparkle. Square corners, no shadows, hairline rules.

STORY: The visitor meets Sara, reads what she wants to write, sees her projects and what she brings, and leaves by requesting a script, writing to her or downloading her CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Peach. Slugline INT. PORTFOLIO — DÍA top left. SARA ABREU in cobalt caps at ~8vw with the role beneath. A cobalt screenplay page drops in at the right and types itself out in Courier from real profile copy, then tilts toward the pointer and lifts with scroll. Three actions sit under the name: projects, CV, contact.

FORM: User-pinned reference world (no seed key: the roll was skipped because the user pinned the world).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment

The screenplay page that types itself beside the name; sluglines in Courier opening every band.

## Constraints

No dark mode. Real content from the reference (bio, ESCAC, experience, interests, capabilities, ¿Por qué Gestmusic?). Scripts and CV PDFs remain placeholders. No film stills or stock photos from the reference.
